# Unit sprite sources

The PNG sprites in this folder are renders of the 3D models below, repainted per side and drawn with three.js (orthographic camera, no texture changes besides colour multiplies). The original model files are not included.

| Sprite | Model | Source | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `arm_*.png` | `public/city/tank/tank.glb` | https://github.com/linranff/GTA_SZ | MIT (below) |
| `air_*.png` | `jet-f16.glb` | munim-maps-vehicles 0.5.0 (npm), generated by munim-maps `scripts/vehicles/make-vehicles.swift` | Apache-2.0 |
| `heli_*.png` | `heli-light.glb` | munim-maps-vehicles 0.5.0 (npm) | Apache-2.0 |
| `inf_*.png` | "Vanguard" soldier (`Soldier.glb`, as shipped in the three.js examples; copy from https://github.com/Orillusion/assets) | Mixamo (Adobe) | Mixamo terms: characters may be used royalty-free in projects; only rendered images are included here |
